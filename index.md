# Zevra Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

## Information We Collect
We collect information you provide when creating an account (email address), wardrobe items and outfit data you add to the app, and photos you upload of clothing items.

## How We Use Your Information
We use your information to provide AI-powered outfit suggestions, enable social features like sharing outfits and following other users, and send notifications about activity on your shared outfits.

## Third Party Services
We use the following third-party services: Supabase (data storage), OpenAI (AI features), Remove.bg (background removal), RevenueCat (subscription management), and Expo (app infrastructure).

## Data Storage
Your wardrobe data is stored securely in the cloud and synced across your devices. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Settings screen.
