Zevra Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Zevra ("Zevra", "we", "us") is an AI outfit-planning app. It lets you build a digital closet, get outfit ideas, rate outfits, and share looks with other people. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We have tried to write it in plain language.

The short version
  • We collect what is needed to run your account, your closet, and the social features you choose to use.
  • When you use AI features, the photos and text for that request are sent to AI providers (mainly OpenAI). We ask for your consent in the app before this happens for the first time.
  • Things you share to the feed, your profile, and your comments are visible to other users. Your liked outfits are private unless you turn that on.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads or track you across other companies' apps.
  • You can delete your account and its data inside the app: Settings, then Delete account.

2. Information we collect

Account information

Your email address, a display name and username, and your password (handled by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text). If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the basic profile details you allow, such as your name and email address (Apple may give us a private relay address instead).

Content you add or create

Photos and details of clothing items in your closet and wishlist, saved outfits, outfit photos you upload for verification or rating, your profile information, and anything you choose to post: shared outfits, descriptions, comments, likes, reposts, follows, and challenge entries.

Things you do in the app

For example which outfits you like or view, who you follow or block, reports you file, notification preferences, and daily usage counters for features that have limits.

Device and technical information

A random identifier generated for your device, your push notification token (if you allow notifications), app version and operating system, and your IP address, which our hosting providers process in the course of delivering the service. If you use the app without an account, this device identifier is how we keep your activity separate from other devices.

Approximate location

If you allow it, or if you enter a city, we use your approximate location only to fetch the weather so outfit suggestions suit the conditions. You can decline location access and still use the app.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Zevra Pro, Apple processes the payment. We (and our subscription provider, RevenueCat) receive your subscription status and a purchase identifier. We never receive your card or payment details.

4. AI features and AI providers

Zevra uses artificial intelligence to understand your clothing photos, suggest and rate outfits, and check that an outfit photo matches the items it should contain. To do this, the content of your request is sent to third-party AI providers for processing:

  • OpenAI receives the photos, item details, and text needed for the request you make, and the text of comments and outfit names or descriptions you post so they can be checked for inappropriate content. Under OpenAI's current API terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default.
  • Background-removal services (we may use Replicate, remove.bg, or Photoroom, through our own server) receive a clothing photo when you ask Zevra to cut an item out of its background.

We ask for your explicit consent in the app before any of your content is sent to an AI provider for the first time, and you can withdraw that consent later in the app's Settings. If you do not consent, the AI features will not work, but the rest of the app will. AI results can be wrong or unexpected; please treat them as suggestions.

Legal and safety

We may disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request, or when needed to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or Zevra. If the business is ever sold or reorganised, your information may be transferred as part of that, and we will tell you if that changes how your data is handled.

6. International transfers

Some of our providers are located outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider participates.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account, closet, and content data: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • After you delete your account, we delete your data from our live systems. Copies in backups may remain for a limited period (up to about 30 days) until they are overwritten.
  • Reports and records we need for safety, abuse prevention, or legal compliance may be kept for as long as needed for that purpose.
  • Content you posted that others have reposted is removed together with your account's content.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the content linked to it, such as your closet, saved outfits, shared outfits, comments, likes, follows, and uploaded photos. This cannot be undone. A small amount of technical or anonymous data that cannot be linked back to your account (for example a device-level record, or anonymous activity) may remain. If you cannot access the app, email SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE from your account's email address and we will delete it for you.

If you signed up with Apple, you can also remove Zevra's access in your Apple ID settings, under Sign in with Apple. Subscriptions are managed by Apple; deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it in your Apple ID settings first.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data (most of it you can edit directly in the app);
  • delete your data (in the app, or by contacting us);
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, such as AI processing consent, without affecting what happened before.

To use these rights, email SUPPORT_EMAIL_HERE. We may need to verify it is you. If you believe we have handled your data unlawfully, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. In Slovenia, this is the Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec, www.ip-rs.si).

10. Your choices in the app

  • AI consent: you can review or withdraw it in Settings.
  • Liked outfits: private by default; make them public or private in Settings.
  • Blocking and reporting: use the "..." menu on outfits, comments, and profiles. Manage blocked users in Settings, Privacy.
  • Notifications and location: manage them in your iPhone's Settings app at any time.

11. Children

Zevra is not intended for children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country for using online services without a parent's consent (for example 15 in Slovenia). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
